Mac 2 | Standby certificate signing and verification

Mac 2 signed the standby CSR with the team CA and staged the signed certificate
and public CA certificate for transfer.

$ openssl verify -CAfile <team-ca-file> standby.crt
standby.crt: OK

$ openssl x509 -in standby.crt -noout -ext subjectAltName
X509v3 Subject Alternative Name:
    DNS:<app-host>, DNS:<api-host>
