Extensions A and B | Mac 2 DNS observations

Redacted dig and OS lookup excerpts. Address labels refer to the main edge.

$ dig @<primary-resolver> <app-host> A +noall +answer
;; connection timed out; no servers could be reached
$ dig @<backup-resolver> <app-host> A +noall +answer
<app-host>.  30  IN  A  <old-edge-address>
$ dscacheutil -q host -a name <app-host>
ip_address: <old-edge-address>

Later, after both resolvers were updated and the OS cache was flushed:
$ dig @<primary-resolver> <app-host> A +noall +answer
<app-host>.  30  IN  A  <current-main-edge-address>
$ dig @<backup-resolver> <app-host> A +noall +answer
<app-host>.  30  IN  A  <current-main-edge-address>
$ dscacheutil -q host -a name <app-host>
ip_address: <current-main-edge-address>
